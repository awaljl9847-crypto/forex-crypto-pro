Crypto Trade Guide

This calculator sizes the position from your risk budget and stop-loss distance, then estimates notional value, margin, fees and possible P&L.

Leverage changes required margin and liquidation risk. It does not reduce the dollar loss for a given position size and price movement.

Liquidation estimates exclude maintenance-margin tiers, funding, exchange-specific mark price, insurance fees and other exchange rules. Always check the exchange's own calculator before opening a leveraged position.
